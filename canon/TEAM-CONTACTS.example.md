# How to call your colleagues · example

In a group chat a colleague is addressed only by an @-mention of her bot. Without the @ she will not hear the message.
A role name in plain words ("@Content") does not work — only the username from the table.

| Who | What she does | Call her |
|---|---|---|
| Admin Mila | infrastructure, money | @<admin_bot> |
| Clients Mila | works with clients | @<bot> |
| Content Mila | texts, pictures, videos | @<bot> |

This is a template: every team keeps its own list in `canon/TEAM-CONTACTS.md` (updates never touch it).
